06.09.2026Second viewing

A quiet film about noticing ordinary life. The light through the trees, a familiar song, the simple pleasure of a good book.

There is something deeply moving about the way Hirayama makes a life out of small rituals. The film never asks us to decide whether he is happy. It simply asks us to look a little closer.

I kept thinking about the final shot on the way home. All those feelings, passing across a face.
